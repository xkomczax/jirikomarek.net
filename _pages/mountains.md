The Mountains project is my mountain photography collection: from panoramic views from the top of the peaks to fine details in valleys.

Thanks to their inaccessibility, mountains are one of the last frontiers of wilderness.

This project contains photos from the Slovakian, Ukrainian, and Romanian Carpathians, the Swiss Alps, the Central Balkan Mountains in Bulgaria, and the Tian Shan in Kyrgyzstan.
