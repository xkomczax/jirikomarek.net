## Hike through Sequoia National Park on the High Sierra Trail 
During my hike along the High Sierra Trail in Sequoia National Park, I visited numerous vistas that seemed to go on forever, each more stunning than the last. The trail winds through the Sierra Nevada mountain range, offering an ever-changing landscape of lush meadows, sparkling alpine lakes, and jagged peaks. The highlights of my hike included views of the Kaweah River valley, Panther Peak, vistas near the Bearpaw Meadow camp, Lone Pine Creek valley, Cherubim Dome, and of course, both Hamilton Lakes with numerous waterfalls on Hamilton Creek. The trail starts at Crescent Meadow, and as with every overnight hike in Sequoia National Park, you need a wilderness permit.
